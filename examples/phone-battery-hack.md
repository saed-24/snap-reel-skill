# Example Output — Phone Battery Hack

Niche tag: tech
Format: Faceless POV + sensory hack (~25 seconds)

## Hook — 0:00–0:02
Extreme close-up, thumb already mid-swipe on the battery settings screen. No face. On-screen text: "Wait… you could just do THIS?" Trending sound at full volume. No talking.

## Normal way — 0:02–0:05
Three fast cuts: closing every app manually, toggling low power mode, dimming the screen. Deliberately tedious. Text: "the way everyone does it 🙃"

## The hack — 0:05–0:12
One continuous shot, thumb only. Tap Settings, then Battery, then the single toggle that does all three at once. Cut every 2–3 seconds, slight zoom each cut. Text: "actually you can just →"

## Payoff — 0:12–0:18
Hold on the battery percentage climbing. Slow reveal. Text: "in under 10 seconds"

## Rewatch bait — 0:18–0:23
Quick montage replaying the toggle from a different angle. Text: "watch it again 👀" Sound loops seamlessly.

## Final frame — 0:23–0:25
Thumb taps the result twice. Text: "save this before it's gone" Sound cuts clean.
