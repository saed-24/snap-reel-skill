# Snapchat Spotlight Trends & Best Practices
**Last updated: 2026-11-23**

## Platform / Algorithm Changes (2026)

- **AI content policy**: Wholly AI-generated videos are no longer recommended in Spotlight. Snap prioritizes authentic human-made content. Content enhanced with Snapchat’s own AI tools remains eligible (with transparency indicators). This shift contributed to +130% YoY growth in Spotlight posters and +40% daily active viewers (Q3 2026 earnings). Sources: [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-snapchat-removed-ai-made-spotlight-posts-posters-up-130/), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/snapchat-will-no-longer-recommend-ai-slop/826782/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/973792/snapchat-will-no-longer-recommend-wholly-ai-generated-videos-in-its-vertical-video-feed).
- **Monetization**: Videos must be ≥30 seconds to earn ad revenue. From 7 May 2026, creators need ≥100 hours Total Spotlight View Time over the last 28 days for maximum Creator Rewards. Program is invite-only: 50k followers + 15k hours view time (last 28 days, ≥3k from Spotlight) + Snap Star + advertiser-friendly original content. Sources: [Snapchat Support – Monetization Program](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/14669003687444-About-Snapchat-s-Monetization-Program), [Snapchat Support – 100-hour tracking](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/47748552848660-How-do-I-track-my-100-Hours-of-Spotlight-View-Time-Last-28-days), multiple creator guides (Lilach Bullock, ShortSync, Clyper).
- **Official ranking signals** (unchanged core): Watch time, completion, favorites, shares, comments, subscriptions, content category, metadata (language, location, hashtags), demographic matching (country/language/age/gender). Skip/hide/report are negative. Source: [Snapchat Support – How We Rank Content](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/8961653169940-How-We-Rank-Content-on-Spotlight).

## New Creator Discovery Tools (Nov 18, 2026)

- **Visited By (Snap Map, global)**: When a creator tags a location in a public Spotlight post (or posts directly to Snap Map), the place can be highlighted on Snap Map for that creator’s followers. Followers see the creator has been there and can open the related post or share the spot via Chat/Stories. Snap Map has 450M+ monthly users. Useful secondary discovery path for local/review content. Sources: [Mashable](https://mashable.com/life/snapchat-new-creator-tools-snap-map-brand-deals), [The Keyword](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/snapchat-visited-by-snap-map), [NBK Social](https://www.nbksocial.com/journal/snapchat-opens-snap-creator-network-in-beta-and-launches-visited-by-and-a/).
- **Snap Creator Network (open beta)**: AI tool for brands to describe desired audience, category, or campaign goal and receive creator matches (emphasizes audience fit over pure follower count). Brands can review performance, shortlist, message, or boost organic content. Creators opt in via Brand Partnerships in Public Profile settings. Sources: [Mashable](https://mashable.com/life/snapchat-new-creator-tools-snap-map-brand-deals), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/snapchat-adds-more-creator-partnership-opportunities/875141/), [The Keyword](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/snap-creator-network-open-beta).
- **Creator Agency Partner Program**: Tiered program (Standard / Strategic) for agencies running Snapchat creator campaigns; rolled out in US, UK, and MENA. Source: same coverage as above.

## Format Stats & Distribution (2026)

- **Length for reach**: 15–30 seconds is the consensus sweet spot for completion and distribution. Shorter improves completion; longer (up to 60s) can work with strong loops/rewatches. Retention drops sharply past ~30s for most content. Sources: [ShortSync 2026 guide](https://www.shortsync.app/guides/snapchat-spotlight-best-practices-2026), [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-use-snapchat-spotlight-to-grow/), [Searchlight Social](https://searchlightsocial.com/snapchat-spotlight-algorithm/).
- **Views**: No reliable public median published. Viral examples routinely hit hundreds of thousands to low millions. Fresh content (<24h) share of US Spotlight views rose >300% YoY. Source: Snap earnings commentary via industry coverage.
- **Native requirements**: Vertical 9:16 full-frame, sound on, no other-platform watermarks, original (not heavily reposted). Native/trending Snapchat sounds and lenses outperform imported audio.

## Hook Formulas & Retention Signals

- **Hook window**: First 1–2 seconds. Use motion, unexpected visual, result-first / payoff-in-frame, bold on-screen text (critical for mute autoplay), pattern interrupt, or clear promise. Avoid slow intros or logo cards. Sources: ShortSync, Searchlight Social, npprteam 2026 playbook, OpusClip captions guide.
- **Key retention signals**: Completion rate, per-user replays/loops, friend shares (DMs), saves/favorites, low early swipe rate. Secondary: profile visits, comments. Edit on a 2–3 second segment grid; end with clear payoff + rewatch bait/loop.
- **Captions**: Burned-in, high-contrast, center or center-bottom, readable in first 1–2s. Majority of early viewers watch muted.

## Posting & Practical Best Practices

- Consistency: 3–5× per week minimum (daily if sustainable). Algorithm needs ongoing data.
- One idea per video. Faceless POV, hands-only, sensory/hacks, before-after, and result-first still perform strongly.
- Use #topic hashtags for classification.
- Cross-post clean verticals, but prioritize native Snapchat elements for Spotlight distribution.
- Location-tag local/review content when relevant to enable Visited By discovery.
